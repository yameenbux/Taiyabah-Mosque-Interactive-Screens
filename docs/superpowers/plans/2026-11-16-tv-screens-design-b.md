# TV screens, design B: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Rebuild all four TV pages (`timetable.html`, `timetablevertical.html`,
`foyer.html`, `foyervertical.html`) to design B, generated from one source by
`build.py`, carrying the 2026 and 2027 timetables.

**Architecture:** The hand-written source lives in `src/`: shared tokens, fonts,
frame and time logic, plus one folder per screen. `build.py` (Python 3,
standard library only) inlines fonts, images, CSS, JS, timetable data and the
fallback foyer copy into four self-contained HTML files at the repo root. Their
names and URLs are unchanged. Orientation is a class on `<body>`. Time logic
lives in plain functions in `src/shared/times.js`, so Node can test it without
a browser. Page behaviour is tested in Playwright against the built files,
served over HTTP.

**Tech Stack:** HTML/CSS/vanilla JS (the screens' existing level: `const`,
arrow functions and template literals are fine; no modules, no build-time
transpile), Python 3 stdlib, Node 22 `node:test`, Playwright (pre-installed),
`jsqr` + `pngjs` (dev only, for decoding QR codes in tests).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-prayer-hall-screen-design.md`.
Approved mockups: timetable B, and foyer b6 + Ramadan from the 16 Nov
conversation.

## Global Constraints

- Output files keep their names, at the repo root. Each starts with `<!-- GENERATED by build.py from src/ — do not edit by hand -->`.
- No network needed to show times. The only requests a built page may make are: the guarded-reload fetch of its own URL, and (foyer only) `foyer-content.json`.
- Every time is written `3:00 pm`: 12-hour, no leading zero, lowercase am/pm in its own smaller `<span class="ampm">`.
- No ticking seconds anywhere. Countdown: `in 1h 54m`, under an hour `in 23m`, in the last minute `now`.
- Jumuʿah times are always labelled `1st` and `2nd`, never joined with `&`.
- Hijri dates are shown exactly as in the data (e.g. `6 Jumada al-Thani 1448 AH`).
- Every stylesheet opens with `[hidden]{display:none!important}`.
- Colours: plum `#3C0B2A`, deep `#24091A`, gold `#C6A24C`/`#DCBB63`, cream `#F3EFE3`/`#FCFAF3`, ink `#261B22`. Fonts: Fraunces, Hanken Grotesk, Amiri, from the app repo's `fonts/` (latin + latin-ext + arabic).
- The store badges are copied unmodified from the app repo's `docs/store-assets/poster/badges/`. They are only ever scaled whole.
- No human face in any asset.
- The donate QR code is the only QR code. It is drawn as a crisp vector path, ink on a cream tile, with a quiet zone of at least 3 modules.
- Reference geometry: 1920×1080 (landscape), 1080×1920 (portrait). Fit-to-viewport scales down only, never up.

## Review Focus

1. **Midnight and the Isha window:** at 00:30 the next jamāʿah is today's Fajr; all cards are unfaded (a new day). Test: `logic.test.mjs › after midnight`.
2. **A Friday after 1st Jumuʿah but before 2nd:** next is Asr, not 2nd Jumuʿah. Test: `logic.test.mjs › friday between jumuah`.
3. **31 Dec 2027 → 1 Jan 2028 (no data):** after Isha jamāʿah on 31 Dec the pill shows nothing, never a wrong time. On 1 Jan the no-data message shows. Test: `logic.test.mjs › year end` and `pages.test.mjs › no data`.
4. **Wi-Fi down at 02:00:** the timetable stays on screen. Test: `pages.test.mjs › guarded reload offline`.
5. **A long phase title or need list in `foyer-content.json`:** nothing clips; it scales down. Test: `pages.test.mjs › foyer long content fits`.

### Task 2: Time logic (`src/shared/times.js`)

**Files:** Create `src/shared/times.js`, `tests/logic.test.mjs`.

**Interfaces (all pure; `days` = merged data, `now` = Date):**
- `hm12(hhmm: string) -> {t: string, ap: "am"|"pm"}`, e.g. `"15:00"` gives `{t:"3:00", ap:"pm"}`.
- `dayKey(d: Date) -> "YYYY-MM-DD"` (local time).
- `slotsFor(days, d: Date) -> Array<{key, en, ar, at: Date, jum?: true}>` gives the five jamāʿah slots for that date. On Friday, Zuhr becomes `{key:"zuhr", en:"Jumuʿah", ar:"الجمعة", at: 1st time, jum:true}`. Returns `[]` with no data.
- `nextJamaah(days, now) -> {key, en, ar, at: Date} | null` gives the first slot of today after `now`, else tomorrow's Fajr, else `null`.
- `passedKeys(days, now) -> Set<string>` holds the keys whose jamāʿah time today is ≤ `now`.
- `countdown(ms: number) -> string` returns `"in 1h 54m"`, `"in 23m"` or `"now"` (`ms < 60000`).
- `nextJumuah(days, now) -> {date: Date, first: string, second: string} | null` gives today if Friday and 2nd not yet passed, else the coming Friday with data.
- `arcFraction(days, now) -> number` is 0 at Fajr begins and 1 at Isha begins, clamped to [0,1].
- Exposed as `window.TT_TIMES = {…}` in the browser, and via `module.exports` when `module` exists (for Node tests).

- [ ] **Step 1: Write failing tests** against real data (load both JSONs, merge):
  - `hm12`: `"05:52"` gives `{t:"5:52",ap:"am"}`, `"12:45"` gives `{t:"12:45",ap:"pm"}`, `"00:05"` gives `{t:"12:05",ap:"am"}`.
  - `between jamaahs`: Mon 16 Nov 2026 13:05 → next `asr`, at 15:00; passed = {fajr, zuhr}.
  - `after isha`: 16 Nov 2026 19:31 → next `fajr`, on 17 Nov at 07:00; passed has all five.
  - `after midnight`: 17 Nov 2026 00:30 → next `fajr` at 07:00 the same day; passed is empty.
  - `friday between jumuah`: Fri 20 Nov 2026 13:00 → next `asr`, at 14:45. At 12:00 → next `zuhr` with `en:"Jumuʿah"`, at 12:45.
  - `clock change`: Sun 28 Mar 2027 13:00 → next `zuhr` at 13:45. Sun 31 Oct 2027 13:00 → next `asr` at 15:30.
  - `year end`: 31 Dec 2027 19:00 → `nextJamaah` is `null` (no 2028), and `slotsFor` for 1 Jan 2028 is `[]`.
  - `countdown`: 6 840 000 ms gives `"in 1h 54m"`, 1 380 000 gives `"in 23m"`, 30 000 gives `"now"`.
  - `nextJumuah`: Mon 16 Nov 2026 gives 20 Nov, `12:45`/`13:40`. Fri 20 Nov 14:00 gives 27 Nov.

### Task 3: Shared frame (`frame.css`, `frame.js`, `tokens.css`)

**Files:** Create `src/shared/tokens.css`, `src/shared/frame.css`, `src/shared/frame.js`.

**Interfaces:**
- Markup contract (in each page template): `#clock` (time + `.ampm`), `#greg`, `#hijri`, `#next` (the pill/bar host), `#jum` (Jumuʿah host), `#content` (the scaled block), `.stage` (drifts).
- `frame.js` exports globals: `fmtTime(hhmm) -> html` (`3:00<span class="ampm">pm</span>`), `renderFrame(days, now)` (fills clock, dates, next jamāʿah and Jumuʿah), `fitToViewport()`, `startReliability()`.
- `startReliability()`: a 1 s tick; a stall over 15 s → `guardedReload()`; at 02:00 → `guardedReload()`; `visibilitychange` after a gap → `guardedReload()`; F or double-click toggles fullscreen. `guardedReload()` fetches `location.href` with `cache:"no-store"` and reloads only if the response is OK and its body contains `GENERATED by build.py`. Otherwise it retries in 15 min.
- Drift: `.stage{animation:drift 150s ease-in-out infinite}` with the existing keyframes (±6 px).
